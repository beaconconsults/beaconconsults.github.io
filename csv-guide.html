Data Format Guide

Every column, in and out

Beacon accepts a standard CSV export from your ERP, POS, or inventory system — no ERP access or integration required. This guide covers every column we read from your file, what each output means, and how to get the most accurate analysis from your data.

Column mapping is automatic

We auto-detect your column names on upload. If your columns have custom names, we'll match each field manually with you — you don't need to rename anything before sending your file over.

Output columns

What we calculate and report for each SKU after analysis.

FieldDescriptionExample
